What the platform is built on

Species identification from sound

Recordings are converted to 5-second windows and then to log-mel spectrograms over 64 mel-spaced frequency bands. A logistic regression trained on these arrays separates the calls of each species and is stress-tested by mixing ship noise back into the training audio at signal-to-noise ratios from 20 dB down to 0 dB.

Grad-CAM is used to confirm the network attends to the call structure rather than to artefacts of the background noise. The same feature extraction is implemented identically in Python and in the browser, so on-server and in-browser inference agree exactly.

Forecast and risk

Daily detection records become rolling rate features over 7, 14 and 30 days, combined with seasonal and day-of-year terms. Gradient boosting predicts whether a species will be present in the following seven days, trained only on earlier years.

Ship-strike risk combines that forecast with vessel traffic derived from AIS positions and a logistic lethality curve relating speed to collision probability, which also enables the 10-knot what-if scenario.
