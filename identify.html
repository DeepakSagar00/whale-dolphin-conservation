How to read this result

Photographic identification is a model estimate on reference imagery. It supports triage of large image collections and is not a substitute for identification by a trained observer.

The classifier was trained on reference imagery retrieved from Wikimedia Commons. Select any photograph to load it into the classifier above.

Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons contributors and are reused here under their respective free licences. The classifier reaches about 73% accuracy on held-out images: the baleen whale classes are reliable, while the two dolphin classes are frequently confused with one another because of their visual similarity.
