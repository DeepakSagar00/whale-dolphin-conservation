About the Platform

WhaleAI Conservation is a capstone-built marine conservation platform that combines acoustics, geospatial analysis and operational risk modelling into one working system.

Mission & vision

Make marine protection measurable

Every conservation decision is made with incomplete information. The platform's purpose is to close that gap: to listen to the ocean continuously, understand which animals are present and where, and translate that into advice a vessel captain can act on the same day.

The vision is a shared, open evidence base. Detection records, models and risk outputs are published so that regulators, researchers and conservation organisations are not each rebuilding the same baseline.

Vision

From detection to decision

A single platform that turns passive acoustic monitoring into specific, spatially explicit recommendations — and makes the reasoning behind each recommendation inspectable.

Approach

Open by default

Models, datasets and advisory outputs are published openly so results can be verified and reused rather than re-derived.

Technology

What the platform is built with

Acoustic analysis

Log-mel spectrograms, 5-second windowing and noise-robustness testing against mixed ship noise at 0–20 dB SNR.

Machine learning

PyTorch and scikit-learn for classification, gradient boosting for presence forecasting, and explainability through Grad-CAM.

Geospatial analysis

OBIS occurrence records, H3 hexagonal grid indexing, haversine distance and AIS vessel tracks for traffic exposure.

Experiment tracking

MLflow-compatible run tracking, so every model, metric and parameter stays reproducible and comparable.
