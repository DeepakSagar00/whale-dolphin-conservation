About the platform

WhaleAI Conservation is a capstone-built marine conservation platform that combines acoustics, geospatial analysis and operational risk modelling into one working system.

Mission and vision

Every conservation decision is made with incomplete information. The platform's purpose is to close that gap: to listen to the ocean continuously, understand which animals are present and where, and translate that into advice a vessel captain can act on the same day.

The vision is a shared, open evidence base. Detection records, models and risk outputs are published so regulators, researchers and conservation organisations are not each rebuilding the same baseline.

Technology

  • Acoustic analysis — log-mel spectrograms, 5-second windowing, noise robustness against mixed ship noise at 0–20 dB SNR
  • Machine learning — scikit-learn and PyTorch for classification, gradient boosting for presence forecasting
  • Geospatial analysis — OBIS occurrence records, H3 hexagonal indexing, haversine distance, AIS vessel tracks
  • Data platform — PostgreSQL with a Bronze/Silver/Gold layering and full run traceability
